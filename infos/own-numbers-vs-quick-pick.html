Picking Your Own Numbers vs. Quick Pick: What Do the Odds Say?

With every ticket, the same question comes up: should you pick your own numbers or let the machine do it with Quick Pick? Many players have a strong preference, but what do the odds really say? Here is a clear and honest comparison of the two approaches.

What Is Quick Pick?

Quick Pick is a random selection made by the sales terminal. You do not have to mark anything: the machine generates a combination at random for you. You can ask the clerk for it or mark the QP box on the playslip.

What the Odds Say

In Fantasy 5, every combination of 5 numbers out of 36 has exactly a 1 in 376,992 chance of being drawn. That holds for your personal combination just as it does for a Quick Pick one. Whether you choose "3-8-14-22-31" or "1-2-3-4-5", your chance of winning is strictly the same.

The Birthday Trap

Many players choose birthdays or anniversaries, which concentrates their numbers between 1 and 31. Those combinations are therefore played by lots of people. If one of them is drawn, the jackpot may be split among several winners. This does not change your chance of winning, but it can reduce your payout.

Why So Many Winners Use Quick Pick

Quick Pick accounts for a large share of tickets sold. It is therefore logical that a large share of winners used it. That does not mean it is more effective, only that it is very common.

How to Pick Your Numbers Wisely

  • Vary your numbers by going above 31 when the game allows it.
  • Avoid obvious sequences such as 1-2-3-4-5.
  • Do not keep changing combinations out of superstition: it improves nothing.
  • Keep your favorite numbers if it makes the game fun, simply.

Common Mistakes

The most common is believing that some method improves your chance of winning. Neither past statistics, nor lucky numbers, nor Quick Pick change the odds. Another mistake is spending more to "multiply" your chances without setting a limit.

Tips for Playing Responsibly

Choose the method you enjoy, but always keep a precise budget. Playing should remain a pastime, never a source of income. If you notice you are spending more than planned, take a break.

Can You Combine Both?

Yes. Some players choose a few personal numbers and let the terminal fill in the rest at random. This mixed method does not change your odds, but it adds a personal touch to the game while limiting the risk of playing overly common combinations.

An Important Reminder

Gambling should remain a pastime. Spend only what you can afford to lose, avoid playing when emotional or tired, and review your spending regularly. If playing starts to cause stress or financial difficulty, talk to someone you trust or to a specialized support service. It is reserved for adults.

Why Sharing the Jackpot Matters

In Fantasy 5, the highest prizes are shared among all the winners of a single drawing. If you play very common numbers, such as birthdays, you raise the chance of sharing the jackpot without raising the chance of winning it. Playing less popular numbers does not make a win more likely, but it can make it more generous.

Summary

Statistically, your numbers and Quick Pick have exactly the same chances. The only real difference is the risk of sharing a jackpot with other players. Choose whatever suits you, and play in moderation.
