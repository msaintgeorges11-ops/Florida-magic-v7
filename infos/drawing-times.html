Florida Lottery Drawing Times (Midday and Evening)

Knowing what time the Florida Lottery drawings take place is the first thing to check before buying a ticket. If you arrive too late at the counter or validate your play after sales close, your bet will count for the next drawing. This guide explains the Midday and Evening drawing times, the difference between the two, how to convert these times for your country and how to never miss a result.

Two drawings a day: Midday and Evening

Florida's daily games, such as Pick 2, Pick 3, Pick 4 and Pick 5, offer two drawings every day, every day of the year. The Midday drawing takes place in the early afternoon, while the Evening drawing takes place late in the evening. Each drawing is independent: it has its own winning numbers, and a ticket is only valid for the drawing chosen at the time of purchase.

Drawing time table (Eastern Time)

DrawingApproximate time (ET)
Midday1:30 PM
Evening9:45 PM

These times are given in U.S. Eastern Time, the reference time zone of the Florida Lottery. Sales close a few minutes before the drawing, so it is wise to buy at least 15 to 20 minutes earlier. Other games, such as Fantasy 5, Cash Pop, Cash4Life, Powerball or Mega Millions, follow their own schedule. Always check it on the official website before playing.

Converting the times for your country

Florida is on Eastern Time, although part of the state's northwest is on Central Time. In Haiti, the time is most of the time the same as in Florida. In France, add about 6 hours: the 1:30 PM ET drawing therefore takes place around 7:30 PM, and the 9:45 PM ET drawing around 3:45 AM the next day. Note: daylight saving changes in Europe and the United States do not fall on the same dates, and for a few weeks each year the gap becomes 5 hours. In Spain, the rule is the same as in France.

Why the time matters

A regular player organizes their bets around the drawings. If you follow statistics, for example the numbers that come up most often, you need to know precisely whether your ticket is for the Midday or the Evening drawing. A mix-up can make you think you lost when you simply played a different drawing. Also, a ticket bought too late is automatically assigned to the next drawing, which completely changes your chances of winning that day.

Practical tips to never miss anything

  • Set an alarm on your phone 30 minutes before each drawing.
  • Check the Midday or Evening label printed on your ticket.
  • Keep your ticket in a safe place until the result is verified.
  • Always cross-check the numbers with an official source.

What if you miss a drawing?

Don't panic: the winning numbers can be checked after the drawing, and your ticket remains valid for the drawing it states. Simply check the results as soon as possible, because prizes must be claimed within a limited period. If you planned to play and missed the sales cutoff, don't try to make up for it by betting more on the next drawing: always stick to your original budget.

In summary

Remember two simple reference points: a Midday drawing around 1:30 PM and an Evening drawing around 9:45 PM, Eastern Time. Convert these times to your time zone, buy with a safety margin and check the drawing shown on your ticket. Always play in moderation and within your budget.
