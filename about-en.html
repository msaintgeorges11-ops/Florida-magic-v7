About FLORIDA MAGIC V7

FLORIDA MAGIC V7 is an entertainment website. It brings together in one place a random number generator, a daily horoscope, a summary of the latest Florida Pick 3 and Pick 4 results, and a series of educational guides that explain how these games work and what their real odds are.

Our mission

We want visitors to enjoy a fun experience while also understanding the truth about the lottery: it is a game of chance, there is no method to predict the drawings, and it should be treated purely as entertainment. That is why, alongside the site's tools, we publish content that explains the math behind every play and promotes responsible gaming.

What you will find on the site

  • Luck: every day and for every drawing (midday and evening, Florida time) the site shows 2 lucky numbers, calculated with a formula from the date and the drawing. They are the same for every visitor. It also includes a "spin" game with a visual effect.
  • Astral: a daily horoscope for the 12 zodiac signs. Each text includes a number and a color of the day, a message about your day and about love, and can be listened to aloud. The positions of the Moon and the planets are calculated for today's date; their interpretations are traditional and predict nothing.
  • Results: a table with the latest published Florida Pick 3 and Pick 4 results, taken from public sources, as a quick reference.
  • Educational guides: articles in Spanish, French and English about how Pick 3 and Pick 4 work, the difference between Straight and Box, the real odds, how to read the results, the most common myths, responsible gaming, the zodiac signs, lucky numbers and how to read a daily horoscope.
  • VIP access (optional): an additional entertainment section for those who request it via WhatsApp. It is completely optional, is not required to use the site and does not guarantee any result.

How the numbers are calculated

The lucky numbers come from a mathematical formula that uses the date and the drawing as inputs. They are not based on statistics of past results or on any prediction system, because none works: in every drawing, all combinations have exactly the same probability. The numbers on the site are for fun, for example to pick a combination "by gut feeling", but they do not increase your chances of winning.

Our sources and our commitment

For official results, the only reliable source is the Florida Lottery. The results we show may be delayed or contain errors, so they should never be used to decide whether a ticket is a winner. Always check at www.flalottery.com.

We write our guides with verifiable information and in plain language. If you find a mistake or want to suggest a correction, write to us from the Contact page.

Important

The numbers generated are random and for entertainment only. They do not predict any drawing and do not increase your chances of winning. No tool can.

FLORIDA MAGIC V7 is not affiliated with, associated with or endorsed by the Florida Lottery or any other lottery.

Responsible gaming

This site is for adults over 18 only. Play responsibly and never bet money you cannot afford to lose. If you feel gambling is becoming a problem, seek professional help. Read our responsible gaming guide.
