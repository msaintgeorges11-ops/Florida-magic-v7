This conversion matters to Dominicans living in Spain and other European countries, and to European tourists travelling to the Dominican Republic. Many people send euros to their families or pay for hotels and excursions, and want to know how many pesos that amount is worth.

The euro and the dollar do not move the same way against the Dominican peso, so the value of one euro in DOP can change even when the dollar is steady. If you receive a transfer in euros, check the day's rate and the fee charged by the sender's service, because small differences add up on larger amounts.

How euros are converted to Dominican pesos

This calculator builds the EUR/DOP rate by combining the value of the euro and of the peso against the dollar, which is called a cross rate, and then multiplies your euros by it. The swap button shows how many euros equal a given sum in pesos.

Many exchange counters do not price the euro and the peso directly against each other. The person serving you may go through the dollar and apply a margin at each step, so the rate at the counter can end up below the reference rate.

Tips for exchanging euros in the Dominican Republic

  • If you are a tourist, ask which currency your hotel and excursions are charged in: euros, dollars or pesos.
  • When a card terminal offers to charge you in euros or in pesos, choosing pesos and letting your own bank convert is often the better deal. Check with your bank.
  • If you receive euros from Spain or another European country, ask exactly how many pesos you will get, fees included.
  • Compare the final amount in pesos between two services before you send or exchange.
  • Keep the receipt showing the rate applied.

EUR to DOP: frequently asked questions

Why is the euro not worth the same as the dollar in pesos?
They are different currencies and move differently against the peso. Their relationship with the dollar changes with the market, so the value of a euro in pesos moves too.

Does this rate tell me how much a transfer will bring?
It is a reference. The real amount depends on the rate and the fee of the service you use.

How often is it updated?
The latest available rates load each time you open the page, and they update once a day.

Can I convert pesos to euros?
Yes, with the swap button or by choosing DOP as the source currency and EUR as the target.
