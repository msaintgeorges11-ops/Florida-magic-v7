France and other European countries have large Haitian communities, and many families receive money in euros from there. The person receiving it in Haiti needs to know how many gourdes it really is, to compare what they are paid with what the transfer receipt says.

The euro and the dollar have different values and do not move the same way against the gourde; that relationship shifts with the European and Haitian economies. Before you exchange, check the day's rate, ask whether the service charges a separate fee, and avoid exchanging at places that do not show the rate clearly.

How euros are converted to gourdes

This calculator combines the value of the euro and of the gourde against the dollar to get the EUR/HTG rate, then multiplies your amount by it. The swap button shows how many euros you need to reach a given amount in gourdes.

If you are receiving money from France or another European country, your benchmark should be the amount in gourdes you were promised, not just the number of euros. If the sum you are paid is far lower than the figure on this page, ask where the gap comes from: it may be a fee, a margin, or an extra conversion through dollars.

Tips for receiving or exchanging euros in Haiti

  • Ask whether you will be paid in gourdes or in dollars, and at what rate the conversion is made.
  • Ask to see the rate in writing, and avoid places that will not state it.
  • Compare the total you receive in gourdes between at least two services.
  • Check whether the fee is taken out of the amount sent or added on top.
  • Keep the receipt and the transfer code.

EUR to HTG: frequently asked questions

Why do I receive fewer gourdes than I calculated?
The reference rate does not include fees or the margin of the service paying you. That is usually where the gap comes from.

Are the euro and the dollar worth the same against the gourde?
No. They are different currencies and each moves at its own pace against the gourde.

Can euros be received directly in Haiti?
It depends on the service and the bank. Many pay out in gourdes or dollars, so ask before you send.

Can I convert from gourdes to euros?
Yes. Use the swap button or choose HTG as the source currency.
