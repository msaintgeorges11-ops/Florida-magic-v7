Haiti and the Dominican Republic share an island and a lot of trade, especially at the border and in the binational markets. Haitian traders, students and workers who live in or travel to the Dominican side need to know how many Dominican pesos their gourdes are worth.

This page works out the cross rate between the two currencies from their values in dollars, and each exchange office also applies its own margin. That is why it is normal for the rate offered in a border market to differ from the reference rate; compare before you exchange.

How gourdes are converted to Dominican pesos

This calculator works out the HTG/DOP rate from the value of both currencies against the dollar (a cross rate) and multiplies your gourdes by it. The swap button shows how many gourdes equal a given sum in pesos.

Because amounts in gourdes tend to be large, the table here uses bigger sums than other pages, from 100 up to 10,000 gourdes. On larger amounts a small gap in the rate changes the result noticeably, so ask for the rate before you agree to an exchange.

Tips for exchanging gourdes for Dominican pesos

  • Ask for both the buy rate and the sell rate; they are not the same.
  • At border markets, compare with the reference on this page before you accept a rate.
  • Check that notes are not torn, badly worn or counterfeit, and count the money before you leave.
  • If you are exchanging a large sum, do it in a safe place in daylight.
  • Note the rate you were given so you can compare next time.

HTG to DOP: frequently asked questions

Why is the rate at the border different?
Each money changer sets their own margin, and it depends on the moment, the amount and the place. The rate on this page is only a reference.

How is the rate between the gourde and the peso worked out?
The value of each currency against the dollar is compared to get the relation between the two, which is why it is called a cross rate.

Can I convert pesos to gourdes?
Yes. Use the swap button or choose DOP as the source currency and HTG as the target.

How often is it updated?
