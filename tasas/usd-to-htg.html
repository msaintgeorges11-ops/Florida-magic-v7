The US dollar is the most widely used foreign currency in Haiti: many large prices, rents, imports and money sent from abroad are thought of in dollars, while everyday spending is paid in gourdes. That is why USD/HTG is one of the rates the Haitian community checks most, both inside and outside the country.

In daily conversation many Haitians also count in “Haitian dollars”, an informal unit equal to 5 gourdes, which can cause confusion when comparing prices with the real rate. This calculator uses the US dollar and the gourde, so it helps you avoid those mix-ups before you send, receive or exchange money.

How to work out your dollars-to-gourdes exchange

The maths is a single multiplication: amount in dollars × the USD/HTG rate = gourdes. The calculator above does it for you, and the swap button runs it in reverse, from gourdes back to dollars.

Here is a quick way to check any quote you are given: divide the gourdes you are offered by the dollars you are paying. If the result is clearly lower than the rate on this page, the gap is the provider's margin and fees.

Fees matter as much as the rate. If you send 200 dollars and the service takes a 3% fee, the recipient gets the equivalent of 194 dollars, not 200. Always ask how many gourdes will actually arrive.

Tips before you exchange dollars for gourdes

  • Ask for the final amount in gourdes, with every fee and margin already included.
  • Compare at least two options on the same day: a bank, an exchange office and a money-transfer service. Buy and sell rates are never the same.
  • Be wary of a rate that looks far better than the reference rate; there may be hidden fees, limits or conditions.
  • When someone talks about “dollars” in Haiti, check whether they mean US dollars or “Haitian dollars” (5 gourdes each).
  • Keep the receipt or confirmation showing the rate that was applied.

USD to HTG: frequently asked questions

What is a “Haitian dollar”?
It is an informal way of counting used in everyday talk, not a separate currency: one “Haitian dollar” equals 5 gourdes. It is a common source of confusion when comparing prices with the real US dollar rate.

Why is the rate I am offered different from this page?
This page shows a market reference rate. The company changing your money adds its own margin between buying and selling, and sometimes a separate fee.

Does the way the money is delivered change what I get?
It can. Some providers use different rates or fees for bank deposits, cash pickup or mobile wallets, so check each option.

How often is the rate updated?
The latest rates load whenever you open the page, from a data source that updates once a day. It is not meant for minute-by-minute trading.
