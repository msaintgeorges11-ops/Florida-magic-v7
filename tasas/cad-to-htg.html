Canada, especially the Montreal area, has one of the largest Haitian communities outside Haiti, and many people send Canadian dollars to their families. Although it is called a “dollar”, the Canadian dollar is not worth the same as the US dollar, so you cannot use the USD rate to work it out.

If you send or receive money from Canada, always compare how many gourdes each service gives for every Canadian dollar, because fees and exchange margins vary a lot between options. This reference rate gives you the starting point.

How Canadian dollars are converted to gourdes

This page uses the CAD/HTG reference rate: multiply your amount in Canadian dollars by that rate to get gourdes. The swap button runs the calculation the other way.

Do not use the US dollar rate to estimate an amount in Canadian dollars. They are different currencies, and their values against the gourde are not the same. If someone says “a hundred dollars”, ask which dollars they mean.

Tips for sending or receiving money from Canada

  • Compare how many gourdes each service pays for every Canadian dollar, fees included.
  • Check whether the service charges a flat fee, a percentage or both, because it changes a lot with the amount.
  • Ask whether the payout in Haiti will be in gourdes or in US dollars.
  • Tell the person receiving the money the exact rate the calculation was based on.
  • Keep the receipt until the money has been delivered.

CAD to HTG: frequently asked questions

Is the Canadian dollar worth the same as the US dollar?
No. Even though they share a name, each has its own value and moves differently.

How many gourdes are 100 Canadian dollars?
Check the quick table or type 100 into the calculator; it uses the day's reference rate.

Why is the amount that arrives lower?
Because fees are taken out and the service applies its own margin on top of the reference rate.

Can I convert from gourdes to Canadian dollars?
Yes, with the swap button.
