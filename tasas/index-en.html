How is the dollar rate determined?

An exchange rate tells you how many units of one currency you need to buy one unit of another. It changes during the day depending on supply and demand in international markets, inflation, interest rates and each country's economic situation.

This page uses international reference rates. Banks, exchange offices and money transfer companies apply their own buy and sell margins, so the amount you receive at a counter may differ. Always compare several options before exchanging money.

Frequently asked questions

How often are the rates updated?
Rates are loaded every time you open the page and come from a data source that is updated daily.

Why is the bank's rate different?
Banks set separate buy and sell prices and add fees. This page shows a market reference rate.

Can I use this calculator for Dominican pesos and gourdes?
Yes. Choose DOP or HTG in the selectors and the conversion is calculated instantly.
