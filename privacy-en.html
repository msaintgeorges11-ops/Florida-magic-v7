Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

At FLORIDA MAGIC V7 we respect your privacy. This policy explains what information is used when you visit floridamagicv7.com and how it is handled.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account or give us your name, address or payment details on the site. The information that may be generated is:

  • Technical data about the visit, such as IP address, browser type, device and pages visited.
  • The VIP code, only if you choose to enter it to activate VIP content.
  • Messages you voluntarily send us via WhatsApp or email.

2. How we use the information

  • To display the site and its features (entertainment numbers, results, horoscope).
  • To verify a VIP code when you enter it.
  • To answer your questions and provide support.
  • To keep the site secure and improve how it works.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Browser storage and cookies

The site saves some preferences in your own browser, for example the language you chose and, if you enable it, the VIP access status. This data stays on your device and you can delete it from your browser settings. The site may also use a service worker to load faster and work on a poor connection.

The third-party services described below, such as advertising, may use their own cookies.

4. Advertising (Google AdSense)

This site may display Google AdSense ads. Google and its partners, as third-party vendors, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. The use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to show you ads based on your browsing.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising at adssettings.google.com.
  • More information on how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Depending on the region where you are located, a notice may appear asking you to accept or reject the use of advertising cookies.

5. VIP codes

When you enter a VIP code, it is sent to our server only to check whether it is valid. We do not use that code for any other purpose. To get a code or support, contact us via WhatsApp.

6. Hosting and third-party services

The site is hosted on Vercel and may display results taken from public sources. These services may record technical data such as your IP address or browser type, according to their own policies.

7. Minors

This site is intended only for adults over 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.
