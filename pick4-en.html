How Florida Pick 4 Works

Pick 4 is the "big brother" of Pick 3 in the Florida Lottery. Instead of three digits you choose four, from 0 to 9. That makes winning harder, but the prizes are much higher. Here is how it works, the play types and the real odds.

When the draws take place

Like Pick 3, Pick 4 has two draws a day: midday (around 1:30 PM Eastern Time) and evening (around 9:45 PM). Each draw is independent of the previous ones.

How to play

The player chooses four digits, for example 2-8-8-5, the wager amount and the type of play. In the draw, one digit is drawn for each of the four positions. Because digits can repeat, there are exactly 10,000 possible combinations, from 0000 to 9999.

Play types

  • Straight (exact): all four digits must come out in the same order. It has the highest prize.
  • Box: you win if the four digits come out in any order. The prize is lower, but the odds are better.
  • Straight/Box: mixes both; you collect more if it comes out in exact order and the box prize if it comes out in another order.

In box play, the prize depends on how many distinct arrangements your number has. A number with four different digits (like 1-2-3-4) has 24 arrangements; one with a repeated digit (1-1-2-3) has 12; with two pairs (1-1-2-2) has 6; and with three alike (1-1-1-2) has 4. The fewer the arrangements, the higher the prize, because it is harder to hit.

The real odds

These figures are mathematical and do not change with the day, the hour or the date. Exact prize amounts are published by the Florida Lottery at www.flalottery.com.

Common mistakes

  • Believing in a pattern: draws use random selection equipment. Looking at past results is interesting, but it cannot predict the next one.
  • Chasing losses: raising your bet to "win it back" is the fastest way to overspend.
  • Trusting promises of results: if someone assures you they know which number will come out, they are not telling the truth.

Play responsibly

Treat the lottery only as entertainment. Decide in advance how much you are willing to spend, do not use money for essential expenses and stop playing if it stops being fun. Only people 18 or older may play. If gambling is causing you problems, seek professional support.
